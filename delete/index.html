Delete your StaMina account

Slet din StaMina-konto

EnglishDansk

English

In the app: You → Settings → Account and backup → Delete account and data. Type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app: e-mail deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com from the Google account you use in StaMina, with the subject "Delete my StaMina account".

We delete your account, plans, check-ins, runs, routes and consents from our database within 30 days. Encrypted backups roll off within a further 8 weeks. A short record that the deletion happened (no personal data) and the consent log are kept as proof. See the privacy notice.

Dansk

I appen: Dig → Indstillinger → Konto og backup → Slet konto og data. Skriv SLET for at bekræfte.

Uden appen: skriv til deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com fra den Google-konto, du bruger i StaMina, med emnet "Slet min StaMina-konto".

Vi sletter din konto, planer, check-ins, løb, ruter og samtykker i vores database inden for 30 dage. Krypterede backups forsvinder inden for yderligere 8 uger. En kort registrering af, at sletningen er sket (uden persondata), og samtykkeloggen gemmes som dokumentation. Se privatlivspolitikken.
