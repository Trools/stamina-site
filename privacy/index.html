English

Version 0.2 – valid from 4 October 2026

Version 0.2 changes only the app name, from StaMina to Minastic. Nothing else has changed.

Who we are

Minastic is a running coach app. The data controller is Troels Rasmussen, Denmark (private person, no company yet). Contact: deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhyLegal basis
Google account id, e-mail, nameSign-in and your accountContract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b))
Birth year, sex, weight, goals, available days, other sportsTo build your training plan (age and sex adjust heart-rate and pace estimates; weight is used for load estimates)Contract; weight together with health data: your explicit consent
Heart rate (max, resting, during runs), check-ins (energy, soreness, pain, illness)To adjust your plan safelyYour explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a))

Optional: use of pseudonymised data to train models that improve Minastic's plansOnly if you turn it onYour explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a))

We do not collect your CPR number. We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Where your data is stored

On your phone and watch, and in our database at Supabase (our processor) in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Supabase is a US company; support staff and some sub-processors may access data from outside the EU. This is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage): processor, EU storage.
  • Google: you sign in with your Google account. Google handles the sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • OpenFreeMap (Hungary, via Cloudflare): when the app shows a run map, it loads map tiles; this sends your IP address and the map area. No account data is sent.

No one else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. After you delete your account we delete your data within 30 days; encrypted backups roll off within a further 8 weeks. The consent log is kept for 3 years after deletion as proof. If you do not sign in for 24 months, we warn you and then delete the account.

Your rights

You can see, export, correct and delete your data, withdraw consent, object and ask us to restrict processing. Write to deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com. We answer within one month. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing before the withdrawal. You can complain to Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

Withdrawing consent

In Settings, one tap. If you withdraw the health-data consent, we delete your data from the cloud and backup stops. Your data stays on your phone. The app asks you to confirm first. If you turn off routes or model training, we delete the routes from the cloud or stop using your data for training.

Age

Minastic is for people aged 16 and over.

Training guidance

Minastic gives training guidance. Use it at your own risk and seek professional advice when in doubt. It does not predict, prevent or diagnose injuries.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before we change this notice in a way that matters.

Vi indsamler ikke dit CPR-nummer. Vi sælger ikke data, viser ikke reklamer og bruger ingen analyse eller sporing.

Hvor dine data opbevares

På din telefon og dit ur og i vores database hos Supabase (vores databehandler) i Frankfurt, Tyskland (EU). Supabase er et amerikansk firma; support og visse underdatabehandlere kan få adgang uden for EU. Det er dækket af EU's standardkontraktbestemmelser.

Hvem ellers modtager data

  • Supabase (database, log ind, fillagring): databehandler, opbevaring i EU.
  • Google: du logger ind med din Google-konto. Google håndterer log ind efter sin egen privatlivspolitik.
  • OpenFreeMap (Ungarn, via Cloudflare): når appen viser et kort over et løb, henter den kortfliser; det sender din IP-adresse og kortområdet. Ingen kontodata sendes.

Ingen andre, medmindre loven kræver det.

Hvor længe vi gemmer data

Så længe din konto findes. Når du sletter din konto, sletter vi dine data inden for 30 dage; krypterede backups forsvinder inden for yderligere 8 uger. Samtykkeloggen gemmes i 3 år efter sletning som dokumentation. Logger du ikke ind i 24 måneder, advarer vi dig og sletter derefter kontoen.

Dine rettigheder

Du kan se, eksportere, rette og slette dine data, trække samtykke tilbage, gøre indsigelse og bede os begrænse behandlingen. Skriv til deep.beep.studios.contact@gmail.com. Vi svarer inden for en måned. Tilbagetrækning påvirker ikke behandling før tilbagetrækningen. Du kan klage til Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

Træk samtykke tilbage

Under Indstillinger, ét tryk. Trækker du samtykket til helbredsdata tilbage, sletter vi dine data i skyen, og backup stopper. Dine data bliver på din telefon. Appen beder dig bekræfte først. Slår du ruter eller modeltræning fra, sletter vi ruterne i skyen eller stopper brugen af dine data til træning.

Alder

Minastic er for personer på 16 år og derover.

Træningsvejledning

Minastic giver træningsvejledning. Brug den på eget ansvar, og søg professionel rådgivning ved tvivl. Den forudsiger, forebygger eller diagnosticerer ikke skader.

Ændringer

Vi giver besked i appen, før vi ændrer denne tekst på en måde, der har betydning.
